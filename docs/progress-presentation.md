# Harmony — project documentation (single slide)

## Slide text

**Title:** DOCUMENTATION THAT MAKES HARMONY REPRODUCIBLE

- `PROJECT.md` — product goals, architecture, data decisions, and model plan
- `TODO.md` — ordered tasks, owners, milestones, and verification criteria
- Reports — initial EmoPhone results and technical rationale
- ML scripts — repeatable dataset exploration and MuSe preparation
- Privacy — restricted participant data stays local and out of Git

**Footer:** From research evidence to an implementation plan

## Spoken text (about 35 seconds)

“Our documentation is the bridge between the research and the app. The project guide records Harmony’s goals, architecture, data decisions, and model plan. The task list turns that plan into ordered milestones, with owners and a check for each deliverable. Our reports preserve the technical rationale and the initial EmoPhone results, while the machine-learning scripts make key exploration and MuSe preparation repeatable. We also document the privacy boundary: restricted participant data stays on local machines and out of Git. Together, these documents help the team make progress without losing track of evidence, decisions, or next steps.”
